# 文献摘要：Robotics for Warehouses and Logistics (2026)

- 文献：Robotics for Warehouses and Logistics: Technologies, Challenges, and Future Directions
- 作者：Lucia Pallottino
- 期刊：Annual Review of Control, Robotics, and Autonomous Systems
- Volume 9 (2026), pp. 377–401
- 获取日期：2026-09-30
- URL：https://www.annualreviews.org/content/journals/10.1146/annurev-control-032724-020213
- 可信度：高

## 已确认事实

综述覆盖：
- autonomous mobile robots
- SLAM
- path planning
- perception / manipulation
- multirobot coordination
- task allocation
- fleet management
- human–robot collaboration
- safety standards

提出的重要未来挑战：
- interoperability
- advanced AI integration
- scalability
- robustness in dynamic environments
- economic barriers

## 对本项目支撑

证明仓储/物流机器人不是单机导航问题。

端侧/边缘需求至少存在两个层次：
- robot-local navigation/perception/control
- fleet-level coordination/task allocation

这也是后续区分 onboard compute 和 edge/fleet compute 的依据。
